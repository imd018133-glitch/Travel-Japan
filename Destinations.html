Top Destinations in Japan

Must Visit Locations

Tokyo

Japan's vibrant capital city, famous for skyscrapers, anime culture, shopping districts and nightlife.

Kyoto

Known for beautiful temples, shrines, traditional streets and historic charm.

Mount Fuji

Japan's most iconic mountain and one of the most photographed locations in Asia.

Osaka

Famous for street food, shopping and the beautiful Osaka Castle.

Nara

Home to ancient temples and the famous free-roaming deer park.

Hokkaido

A paradise for snow lovers, nature enthusiasts and winter sports activities.
